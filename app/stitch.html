Our Calling

At Grace & Peace, our calling is rooted in a simple yet profound mandate: to serve and love our community as a reflection of divine compassion. We believe that every individual is a story of grace waiting to be told, and we strive to be the supportive chapter where healing and purpose meet.

Our Vision

"Building a house of prayer for all nations, where every soul finds a home and every heart finds peace."

We look forward to a future where the walls of the church extend into the streets, bringing hope to the hopeless and a tangible sense of belonging to all who seek it.
